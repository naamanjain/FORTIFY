# Demo Scenario

This document defines the intended final demonstration storyline and is not implemented in Phase 0.

1. Observe a synthetic longitudinal personnel history.
2. Contextualize current conditions against personal, cohort, and operational baselines.
3. Show a worsening welfare-risk trajectory with human-readable contributors.
4. Simulate multiple policy-approved interventions.
5. Compare projected welfare-risk improvement with operational disruption and staffing/coverage impact.
6. Reject or mark infeasible any intervention violating critical operational constraints.
7. Present a ranked feasible recommendation to an authorized welfare decision-maker.
8. Record and observe the intervention outcome.

Example synthetic demonstration values such as a current projected strain of 81 and simulated values of 61, 49, and 44 are illustrative only and must never be presented as clinically validated results.

## Phase 12 implementation notes

The final demonstration preparation selects a deterministic synthetic hero case
from the Phase 5-7 outputs **restricted to person-days that the alert policy
actually turned into an open workflow case**. Read the current hero from
`artifacts/phase12/demo_manifest.json` (`hero_case.person_id`,
`hero_case.target_date`, `hero_case.workflow_state_at_preparation`) instead of
hardcoding it here: it changes whenever the dataset is regenerated, and it
changes as soon as a reviewer acts on the case, because
`workflow_state_at_preparation` is read from the workflow store.

Selection criteria: a HIGH band day with `PRIORITY_WELFARE_REVIEW`,
CONSTRAINED or FEASIBLE_WITH_ADJUSTMENT feasibility, at least two constraint
flags, and the largest 21-day rise in model probability among the open cases.
Ties break deterministically on person id and date.

Why the restriction to open cases exists: the hero used to be chosen from the
scoring outputs alone. On the current 500 x 180-day build that selected a
person-day the alert policy had budget-suppressed, so no workflow case existed
for it, the scripted step 2 below could not be performed, and the absent row
was reported as state `NEW` - indistinguishable from a real unreviewed case.

The recommended walkthrough is:

1. Open the Phase 9 dashboard and show the aggregate welfare overview.
2. Open the prepared hero case and show the recent probability/risk trajectory.
3. Show the existing operational contributing signals and Phase 7 feasibility constraints.
4. Use the Phase 10 human workflow panel to acknowledge and review the recommendation.
5. Demonstrate a supported human workflow transition and then show its audit/history evidence.
6. Explicitly state that all demonstration records are synthetic and that the system provides welfare decision support, not clinical diagnosis or autonomous personnel action.

The earlier illustrative simulation values in this document remain illustrative only; Phase 12 does not claim those values are generated by a validated clinical or causal model.
